license, and you keep the copyright in what you write.

## Sign-off

Every commit carries a `Signed-off-by:` line, which `git commit -s` adds. It certifies the
[Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/): you wrote the change, or you
otherwise have the right to submit it under the MPL 2.0.
